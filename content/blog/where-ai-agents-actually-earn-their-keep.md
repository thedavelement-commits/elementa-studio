## Frequent

If a task happens twice a month, automating it is rarely worth it. If it happens twenty times a day, it probably is.

## Repetitive

Agents shine when the right answer is usually the same. Opening hours, prices, delivery times and how to get started are perfect.

### Low risk

Start where a mistake is easy to fix. Leave refunds, legal questions and anything sensitive with a person until the agent has proven itself.

## Score your list

Give each candidate two numbers: hours saved per month and how much damage a wrong answer would cause. Start at the top of the list and build from there.
