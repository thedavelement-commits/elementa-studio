## First impressions are not free

People decide in seconds whether something looks trustworthy. An unfinished look makes a good product feel risky, especially when you are asking for money or personal details.

## Rebranding later is expensive

Changing a brand after launch means new assets, new screenshots, new videos and explaining to existing customers why everything looks different.

### What a launch brand needs

You do not need a hundred page guide. You need a logo that works small and large, two fonts, a short colour palette, a few rules and a social kit you can actually use.

## The simple test

If you would feel proud sending your website to the person you most want as a customer, the brand is ready. If you would apologise first, it is not.
