## Start with the inbox, not the tool

Before choosing any platform, read the last two hundred messages your business received. Group them. You will usually find that a small handful of questions make up most of the volume.

## Decide where a human steps in

Every agent needs a clear line. Anything about money, complaints or something the agent has not seen before goes to a person, with the conversation attached so nobody has to ask twice.

### Measure one thing

Pick a single number for the first month, like how many questions were answered without a person. If that number moves, widen the agent. If it does not, fix the answers before adding more.

## What week one really looks like

By Friday you want an agent that answers the common questions correctly, hands everything else over politely, and gives your team an hour or two back every day. That is a win worth building on.
