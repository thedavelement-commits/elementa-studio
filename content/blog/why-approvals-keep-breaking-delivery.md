## One approver per stage

Many people can comment. One person decides. Agree who that is at the start and write it down.

## Review against the brief, not taste

Every piece of feedback should answer one question: does this do what we agreed it would do? Personal taste matters, but it should not restart the project.

### Fixed rounds keep everyone honest

Two clear revision rounds per stage gives space to improve the work without turning it into an endless loop.

## Make the next step obvious

Every review ends with one line: approved, or approved with these changes. Anything vaguer quietly adds a week.
