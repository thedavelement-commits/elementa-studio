## Days one and two: the script

We write the script before anything is designed. If it does not work as plain words, no animation will save it.

## Days three to five: style frames

A few key frames show the look and feel. Once these are approved, the rest of the video follows the same rules.

### Days six to nine: animation

Motion is built scene by scene, with a review halfway so there are no surprises at the end.

## Day ten: delivery

You get the final video in every size you need, for your website, X, Instagram and ads, ready to post.
